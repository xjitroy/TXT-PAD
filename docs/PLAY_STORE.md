# Google Play submission package

The versioned GitHub release supplies:

- **TXT.aab**: signed Android App Bundle for Play Console (use this for a new release).
- **TXT.apk**: signed universal APK for direct installation/testing outside Play.
- **mapping.txt**: R8 deobfuscation mapping; retain it for crash analysis and upload if requested.
- **release-metadata.json**, **SHA256SUMS**, **signing-certificate.pem**: build identity and verification.
- **TXT-store-assets.zip**: 512×512 opaque icon, 1024×500 feature graphic, phone screenshots,
  English/Bengali listing copy, public privacy policy and this checklist.

Source assets and text are also in `store/`; the original app mark and editable SVGs are included.
Phone screenshots must show this build. App graphics are generated by tools/generate-store-assets.py
from the existing TXT vector mark (CairoSVG is a development-only dependency).

## Owner-only Console work

An APK/AAB is not a Play listing by itself. Sign in to your developer account, create the app,
confirm your identity/account requirements and provide your real developer/support email and
any required address/phone details directly in Play Console. TXT cannot infer these details.
Set category Productivity. Check the final package name before first submission; the source
uses `com.secure.textpad`, and Play must accept it as available or owned by you.

Use the supplied title/description translations. Privacy policy public URL:
https://github.com/xjitroy/TXT-PAD/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
Read the policy and ensure it matches your published app/business details. It is an accessible
public HTML page; a dedicated developer website can be substituted later.

## Data safety and declarations (review against the actual uploaded build)

- TXT collects/transmits no user data to the developer or third parties. No analytics, ads,
  online account or server; no INTERNET/ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permission. Data safety generally
  answers **no data collected/shared** for TXT's own process. User-selected cloud document
  providers, keyboard and Autofill are separate services disclosed in the policy.
- No ads. No in-app payments. No app login account or account deletion flow is needed.
- Optional device authentication is local; document/backup passwords cannot be recovered.
- Complete IARC content rating truthfully as a general-purpose offline editor with no supplied
  social/user-publication service. Do not invent a content-rating certificate or claim a rating
  until the questionnaire assigns it.
- Choose the real target audience. Being easy for beginners does not by itself make this a
  child-directed app. If you deliberately target children, review the applicable Families policy.
- Reviewer instructions: choose a local folder; New File starts editing. App lock is off by
  default and no developer account is required. Lock/backup passwords are created by the reviewer.
- Select the correct Play App Signing arrangement; read RELEASING.md before accepting it.

SDK target is 36. The release checker verifies signatures, no network permissions, ZIP integrity
and 16 KiB alignment of 64-bit native libraries. Play Console's own validation and policy review
remain authoritative and have not been run by this repository workflow. Use internal testing
and device/pre-launch reports before promoting to production. New personal developer accounts
may have additional testing/verification requirements shown by Play Console.

Play Console uses the AAB, not the Android test APK or unsigned candidate.

No Play account credentials or private signing keys belong in the public package. Actual Play
publication, account forms, assigned rating and review approval are completed by the owner.
